# Duet Plus — Release Channel

Public releases for **Duet Plus**, a Nintendo Switch emulator for Android dual-screen handhelds, tuned for stability on the AYN Thor.

Download the latest APK from the [Releases](../../releases) page.

## What it fixes

- **FINAL FANTASY RESONANCE** — crashes, freezes, red-tinted characters, blinking battle HUD and dialogue, missing objects
- **Pokémon Legends: Z-A** — 30 FPS on the Thor without vanishing geometry
- **Double Dragon Revive** — stalls and flickering scenery
- **Dragon Quest VII Reimagined, Fire Emblem Engage** — characters and lights blown out to white

## Requirements

Android 13+ (arm64), a second display for companions, and your own game dumps, keys and firmware. None are included.

Duet Plus installs as its own app (`com.tapionallanaire.duetplus`) and keeps separate data.
